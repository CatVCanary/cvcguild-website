Keep history in people's hands

Give someone a seat at a table four thousand years in the making.

At Cat v. Canary Guild, history isn't locked behind glass. It's a board between two strangers, a volunteer explaining the first move, and the moment someone realizes they can play a game older than kingdoms.

We teach for free. Your generosity helps us bring that experience to the next visitor, the next family, and the next faire.

Your gift has a job to do

You're not funding an abstract idea. You're keeping a real traveling classroom open.

Every season, our all-volunteer Guild packs historical game boards, furnishings, costumes, and teaching materials into trailers and builds a welcoming pavilion where visitors can sit down and learn. Those boards and chairs take wear. Canvas needs repairs. Rain, storage, transport, and accessibility all have real costs.

More seats at the table

Help us expand and maintain comfortable seating, including accessible places for visitors who need them. Our goal is to grow from 22 seats toward 55, with six accessible places.

Games worth preserving

Support sturdy teaching sets of Senet, the Royal Game of Ur, Xiangqi, Hnefatafl, Alquerque, and more—plus replacement pieces, repairs, and storage that protects them between events.

A pavilion that can travel again

Help maintain weather protection, canvas, wooden frames, furniture, safe transport, and storage. We are working toward a more durable pavilion and a lasting home for the collection.

Our volunteers pay no dues and are not expected to buy Guild supplies out of pocket. Your support keeps the educational work sustainable rather than shifting its cost onto the people doing it.

Other ways to put history in motion

Sponsor a practical improvement

Help underwrite seating, a teaching game set, a pavilion repair, or a storage upgrade. We can discuss a specific project and what it needs before you commit.

Donate materials or expertise

Useful woodwork, fabrication, canvas repair, safe storage, transport, and accessibility expertise can make a measurable difference. Please coordinate donations before delivery.

Help us find space

We need suitable temporary workspace for cleaning canvas and restoring furniture, plus secure longer-term storage. Introductions to potential partners are valuable.

Have a little land to share?

We also need a quiet temporary home for our two trailers and restoration work: roughly one usable, flood-safe acre for 2–6 years, ideally donated or at low cost. We wrote a detailed request for potential land hosts, including site use, traffic, insurance, and owner expectations.

A future beyond one season

Help us build something that lasts.

Our aim is not simply to survive the next faire. We want the historical teaching collection, welcoming game house, and knowledge held by our volunteers to outlast any one season or director. A permanent base would protect the collection and make future teaching easier.

Whether you can give a little, offer a useful skill, or introduce us to someone who can help, you become part of that future.
