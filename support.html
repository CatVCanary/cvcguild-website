Support a traveling classroom

Cat v. Canary Foundation keeps historical games tangible and approachable. Our volunteer educators bring boards, pieces, stories, and patient instruction to public events so visitors can learn by playing, without a separate charge for Guild demonstrations.

What gifts support

Our teaching collection travels with us. Equipment and transport costs include game boards and replacement pieces, seating, shade and weather protection, storage, pavilion repairs, and materials that make the space more accessible to visitors.

More ways to help

Financial contributions: Help with maintenance and teaching supplies. Business sponsorship: Discuss support for educational programming and equipment. Workplace giving: Ask whether your employer offers charitable giving or matching programs; availability varies by employer. Donated items: Useful materials and equipment may reduce project costs. Skills and connections: Fabrication, repair, transportation, accessibility expertise, and introductions to potential partners can all help.

Long-term stewardship

We aim to preserve our teaching collection and make the Guild sustainable across future seasons and leadership transitions. Reliable storage and a permanent base would help protect equipment and expand the reach of our public educational work.

Give or get in touch

For current verified donation options, sponsorship inquiries, or in-kind gifts, contact cvcguild@gmail.com. Please use only donation destinations linked directly from our official website or confirmed by the Foundation.
