The Cat v. Canary Guild is a small volunteer company that teaches, sets up and maintains a traveling historical game house. Participation is about stewardship as much as performance: learning a game well enough to teach it, welcoming a visitor, preparing a table, caring for costumes, or making sure another volunteer has eaten and hydrated.

We do not ask volunteers to buy organizational supplies out of pocket, and approved expenses are reimbursed under our policies. A costume lending collection helps newcomers take part without first investing in a private wardrobe. Our regular camp environment is 21+.

Teach and interpret

Study a game, understand the evidence and uncertainty behind its rules, and explain its first moves in a friendly and approachable way.

Create and maintain

Woodworking, sewing, canvas repair, transport, storage, documentation, prop maintenance, and game-piece production all help keep the program sustainable.

Practice hospitality

Assist with setting up comfortable spaces, welcoming visitors, keeping equipment ready, and looking after the people doing the work.

What joining means

A good volunteer experience has boundaries

This is not a paid job or a promise of any particular role, shift, accommodation, or appearance. Volunteer placements depend on available responsibilities, safety and event rules, training, and agreement with Guild policies. We aim to make participation financially approachable and socially respectful while being clear about the real work involved.

There has also been interest from younger potential learners, including ages 10–14. A supervised youth-interest pathway is under consideration, but our normal camp is not open to minors, and we are not advertising a youth program as available yet.
