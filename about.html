About Cat v. Canary Foundation

Cat v. Canary Foundation is a Washington-based 501(c)(3) educational nonprofit (EIN 33-4166947). Our public program, the Cat v. Canary Guild, brings ancient and historical board games to life through free, hands-on teaching at Renaissance faires.

Our mission

We research, recreate, and teach games played by people across centuries and cultures. Our goal is to make history approachable: visitors learn by sitting down, moving the pieces, asking questions, and playing together.

What makes our work different

We bring a furnished, traveling game pavilion to public events, creating a comfortable place to learn and rest. Volunteers teach visitors of different experience levels without charging admission to the Guild's activities. Our game collection reflects traditions from Africa, Asia, Europe, and the Americas.

Hospitality is part of the lesson

Our welcoming space includes seating and shade. We aim to make historical learning accessible and enjoyable for disabled visitors, older adults, artists, and anyone who wants a moment of connection at a busy event.

Our activities are supported by volunteer work, donations, and grants. Donations help maintain our traveling pavilion, teaching games, equipment, and visitor accommodations.
