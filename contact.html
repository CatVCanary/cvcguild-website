What to include in your message

For event invitations, include the event name, location, dates, expected audience, and available space. For educational inquiries, tell us which games or historical periods interest you. For volunteering or donated materials, describe how you would like to contribute.

Ways to support the Foundation

Financial gifts, workplace giving, equipment, professional skills, business sponsorships, and accessibility improvements all support our educational work. Read our support and needs page.
