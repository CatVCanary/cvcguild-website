A good conversation begins with an invitation

For educational demonstrations, event invitations, volunteer questions, donations, sponsorships, donated supplies, or possible land-use arrangements, please email cvcguild@gmail.com. We prefer email for initial conversations, and we do not publish a private residential address as a Guild office.

You can also reach out if you are interested in discussing accessibility for a particular public appearance, the history of a game in our teaching collection, or a connection with another historical or cultural educator.

Event organizers

Include event name, location, proposed dates, audience, footprint, access and logistical requirements, and whether you seek a full traveling pavilion or a smaller demonstration.

Volunteers and collaborators

Tell us the kind of participation that interests you, your relevant skills if any, and the nature of the opportunity. No specialty is required to ask.

Donors and landowners

Describe the proposed gift or the general area and circumstances of any land. Please coordinate privately before sharing sensitive addresses or arranging deliveries.
