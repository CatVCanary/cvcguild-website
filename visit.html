A pavilion of games, not a ticketed attraction of its own

The Cat v. Canary Guild teaches at Renaissance faires and other public living-history events in Washington. Our program is associated with the Washington Midsummer Renaissance Faire near Monroe; for current dates, appearances, venue rules, and admission arrangements, always consult the event organizer and confirm directly with us before making a special trip.

You do not need to know how to play. Look for our illustrated traveling game house and ask a volunteer where to begin. Choose a game, learn the opening moves, and see where the conversation leads.

What to expect

Hands-on historical games, volunteer interpretation, a comfortable setting when space permits, and honest answers about what we know—and do not know—about older rules.

Access needs

Tell us in advance if seating, routes, sensory considerations, or other practical accommodations would make a visit easier. We will explain what we can provide at the particular event.

For event organizers

We welcome serious invitations for educational demonstrations. Please include your dates, location, audience, footprint, logistical support, and relevant exhibitor arrangements.

An honest note on availability

The schedule belongs to the event

We do not publish unconfirmed appearance dates. Individual Renaissance faires manage gate tickets, site maps, parking, accessibility infrastructure, and weather-related schedule changes. The Guild’s teaching itself has no separate admission charge.

For Washington Midsummer Renaissance Faire information, see the official organizer website. For a specific Guild appearance, please write to the Foundation.
