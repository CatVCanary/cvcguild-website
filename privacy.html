Privacy and website information

Cat v. Canary Foundation respects visitors' privacy. This informational website does not require an account to read its public pages.

Contact by email

If you email us, you voluntarily provide your email address and the information in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and manage relevant Guild correspondence.

Online game saves

The online Game House saves game progress in your browser’s local storage where supported. You can clear this data using your browser settings. Clearing site data may remove saved matches.

External services

Links to third-party donation, social, or event websites lead to services with their own privacy practices. Website hosting providers may process ordinary technical access logs. We do not claim control over those services.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests about correspondence you have sent us, contact cvcguild@gmail.com.
