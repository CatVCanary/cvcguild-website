Privacy and website information

This is a public informational website for Cat v. Canary Foundation. No account is required to read these pages. We do not place donation or payment forms on this site; links to payment providers and outside event organizers open their services, which operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

If you email us: Your email address and the information you choose to share are received so volunteers can respond and manage relevant Guild correspondence. Avoid sending sensitive personal details in a first inquiry.

Technical information: When this static website is hosted, the hosting provider may process routine request logs, IP addresses, and device or browser information required to serve the site and maintain service. We do not make claims about retention periods we cannot verify.

Third-party links: Venmo, event websites, employer-giving portals, and other third-party services have independent privacy practices. Please review them before submitting data or payment.

Photography: Public Guild images may show volunteers and visitors. Publication permissions and photographer credits should be reviewed before the site is launched.

Questions: Contact cvcguild@gmail.com about privacy inquiries or correspondence you have sent.

Review status: This version is prepared for director review and is not a statement that a new live hosting or analytics setup has been activated.
