Behind the Curtain

Tales and lore from the traveling house of Cat v. Canary

The following is playful Guild fiction inspired by our real educational mission. It is not presented as historical fact.

Chapter I: A Most Unreasonable Invitation

There was once a pavilion that could be found wherever curious people gathered. It had no permanent throne, no marble staircase, and certainly no royal permission to be as comfortable as it was. Its wealth lay in a hundred small pieces of wood, bone, stone, and cleverness, laid out upon boards that remembered the habits of civilizations.

At its entrance stood a Cat, who believed every table was improved by his presence, and a Canary, who believed every table was improved by his authority. The two agreed on nothing, save one inconvenient principle: a visitor should never be left standing when there was a game to be played.

“I shall explain the rules,” announced the Canary, adjusting a chain of office so magnificent it threatened to outweigh its wearer.

“You shall explain your version of the rules,” replied the Cat, “and I shall explain why you lost.”

From behind a stack of cushions, Bunnie produced a board and quietly invited the visitor to sit. Before either rival finished arguing, the newcomer had made the first move.

Chapter II: The Board with No Single Kingdom

Each board in the pavilion carried a different question. One asked how a traveler might race through an Egyptian landscape of symbols. Another asked how much fortune a Mesopotamian player dared trust. Others set armies on a riverbank, surrounded a fleeing king, or turned three aligned pieces into a small and satisfying triumph.

The Canary declared that he had personally invented strategy. The Cat requested documentary evidence. Bunnie brought out a fresh board and let the visitors decide which claim was more entertaining.

And so the pavilion traveled onward: not as a museum of untouchable treasures, but as a house of games in which history could be handled, questioned, reconstructed, and enjoyed.

What is real, and what is lore?

The Cat, Canary, and Bunnie are fictional Guild characters. Our volunteer-run Foundation, historical game teaching, traveling pavilion, and commitment to welcoming visitors are real.
